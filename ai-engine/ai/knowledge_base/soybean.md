# Soybean

## Scientific Name
Glycine max

## Overview
Soybean is a globally significant oilseed and protein crop. Soybean farming is highly valued because the crop contains about 40% protein and 20% oil. As a legume, Soybean enhances soil fertility by fixing atmospheric nitrogen.

## Suitable Climate
Soybean requires a warm and moist climate.
The ideal temperature for Soybean growth is between 25°C and 30°C.
Soybean requires 600 mm to 850 mm of well-distributed rainfall.
Soybean is highly sensitive to frost and prolonged waterlogging.

## Suitable Soil
Soybean thrives in well-drained, fertile loamy or clay-loam soils.
Soybean requires a soil pH between 6.0 and 7.5.
Soybean cultivation completely fails in highly acidic or saline soils.

## Land Preparation
Plough the Soybean field deeply to ensure a loose seedbed.
Proper leveling of the Soybean field is absolutely critical to prevent water stagnation.
Prepare broad bed furrows (BBF) to provide simultaneous drainage and moisture conservation for Soybean.

## Seed Selection
Select certified Soybean seeds of high-yielding, disease-resistant varieties.
Ensure Soybean seeds have a germination percentage of at least 70%.
Soybean seeds lose viability quickly, so never use seeds older than one year.

## Seed Treatment
Treat Soybean seeds with a fungicide like Thiram or Carboxin to prevent seed rot.
Inoculate Soybean seeds with Bradyrhizobium japonicum bio-fertilizer right before sowing to ensure active nitrogen fixation.

## Sowing Time
In India, Kharif Soybean is sown in June-July right after the first monsoon showers.
Ensure there is adequate moisture in the soil; never sow Soybean in dry soil.
Delayed sowing of Soybean leads to severe yield reductions.

## Spacing
Maintain a row-to-row spacing of 45 cm for Soybean.
Maintain a plant-to-plant spacing of 5 cm to 10 cm for Soybean.
The standard Soybean seed rate is 25 kg to 30 kg per acre.

## Water Requirement
Soybean requires moderate water but is highly sensitive to moisture stress at critical stages.
Soybean requires continuous moisture during flowering and pod-filling.
Soybean plants will immediately drop their flowers if they face severe drought or waterlogging.

## Irrigation Management
Kharif Soybean is largely rainfed, but requires protective irrigation during long dry spells.
The most critical Soybean irrigation stages are flowering (45 days) and pod development (65 days).
Drain excess rainwater from the Soybean field immediately, as stagnation causes root rot.

## Nutrient Requirement
Soybean fixes its own Nitrogen but requires a small starter dose of Nitrogen.
Soybean requires heavy Phosphorus for robust root growth and nodulation.
Soybean requires Potassium and Sulphur for optimal oil synthesis and pod filling.

## Recommended Fertilizer
A standard Soybean fertilizer recommendation is 20 kg Nitrogen, 60 kg Phosphorus, and 40 kg Potassium per hectare.
Apply 20 kg of Sulphur per hectare to the Soybean field to significantly boost oil content.
Apply the entire fertilizer dose as a basal application during Soybean sowing.

## Organic Farming Tips
Use farmyard manure or vermicompost heavily during Soybean land preparation.
Rely on Rhizobium and PSB bio-fertilizers to organically boost Soybean nutrition.
Use Neem oil sprays to deter early pest attacks on the Soybean crop.

## Weed Management
Weeds severely choke the Soybean crop during the first 40 days.
Apply pre-emergence herbicides like Pendimethalin within two days of Soybean sowing.
Apply post-emergence herbicides like Imazethapyr 15 to 20 days after Soybean emergence.

## Crop Rotation
Soybean is an excellent rotation crop that enriches the soil for the next season.
Soybean-Wheat and Soybean-Chickpea are highly successful and widely adopted crop rotations.
Rotating Soybean breaks the life cycle of severe soil-borne pathogens.

## Intercropping
Soybean is frequently used as an intercrop with tall cereals or commercial crops.
Intercrop Soybean with Maize or Sorghum.
Intercrop Soybean with Cotton or Pigeon pea (Tur) for maximum land utilization.

## Major Diseases

### Soybean Rust
Symptoms: Tiny, ash-grey to brown powdery spots appear on the underside of Soybean leaves, causing them to turn yellow and drop prematurely.
Causes: Fungal infection (Phakopsora pachyrhizi).
Prevention: Plant early-maturing Soybean varieties.
Treatment: Spray Hexaconazole or Propiconazole immediately upon detecting Soybean rust.

### Soybean Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV)
Symptoms: Soybean leaves show alternating yellow and green patches. In severe cases, the entire Soybean plant turns yellow, stunting pod formation.
Causes: Viral infection transmitted by Whiteflies.
Prevention: Plant YMV-resistant Soybean varieties.
Treatment: Control the Whitefly vector by spraying Thiamethoxam or Acetamiprid on the Soybean crop.

### Soybean Charcoal Rot
Symptoms: The Soybean plant wilts suddenly in dry weather. The lower stem turns black and the roots decay.
Causes: Fungal infection (Macrophomina phaseolina).
Prevention: Avoid moisture stress. Treat Soybean seeds with Trichoderma.
Treatment: Charcoal rot has no chemical cure once established; maintain proper soil moisture to prevent it.

## Major Pests

### Soybean Girdle Beetle
Symptoms: The adult beetle cuts two parallel rings (girdles) around the Soybean stem or petiole, causing the upper part of the plant to dry and droop.
Prevention: Collect and destroy the girdled Soybean plant parts.
Treatment: Spray Triazophos or Profenofos on the infested Soybean crop.

### Soybean Tobacco Caterpillar (Spodoptera)
Symptoms: Swarms of caterpillars aggressively eat Soybean leaves, completely skeletonizing the plant overnight.
Prevention: Set up pheromone traps to monitor adult Spodoptera moths in the Soybean field.
Treatment: Spray Indoxacarb or Chlorantraniliprole to kill caterpillars on the Soybean crop.

### Soybean Whitefly
Symptoms: Tiny white insects suck sap from Soybean leaves and act as the deadly vector for Yellow Mosaic Virus.
Prevention: Remove weed hosts around the Soybean field.
Treatment: Spray Diafenthiuron or Spiromesifen to control Whiteflies on Soybean.

## Nutrient Deficiency
Nitrogen deficiency causes uniform pale yellowing of the entire Soybean plant.
Phosphorus deficiency causes stunted, dark green Soybean plants with delayed flowering.
Sulphur deficiency causes yellowing of the newest, topmost Soybean leaves.

## Harvesting
Harvest Soybean when the leaves turn yellow and drop off the plant completely.
Harvest Soybean when the pods turn brown or grey and rattle when shaken.
Harvest Soybean when the seed moisture drops to 14-15%. Delaying harvest causes pods to shatter.

## Post Harvest Management
Thresh the Soybean crop carefully using a thresher running at a lower cylinder speed to prevent seed cracking.
Dry the Soybean seeds gently in the sun to bring the moisture content down to 10%.
Clean the Soybean seeds to remove dust and damaged grains.

## Storage
Store Soybean in dry, properly aerated gunny bags.
Do not stack Soybean bags too high, as heavy pressure breaks the fragile seeds.
Soybean loses viability extremely fast in warm, humid storage conditions.

## Expected Yield
A healthy, well-managed Soybean crop yields 2 to 2.5 tonnes per hectare.
High-yielding Soybean varieties under excellent conditions can reach up to 3 tonnes per hectare.

## Common Farmer Mistakes
Using last year's leftover Soybean seeds usually results in massive germination failure.
Sowing Soybean seeds too deep (below 5 cm) prevents the seedlings from emerging.
Ignoring Whitefly populations leads to total crop destruction by Yellow Mosaic Virus.

## Best Farming Practices
Use the Broad Bed Furrow (BBF) or Ridge and Furrow method to perfectly balance drainage and moisture for Soybean.
Always inoculate Soybean seeds with fresh Bradyrhizobium culture just before planting.
Ensure the Soybean field is absolutely weed-free for the first 45 days.

## Government Recommendations
The government strongly advises planting YMV-resistant Soybean varieties in high-risk zones.
ICAR recommends utilizing deeply placed basal fertilizers to encourage deep Soybean root growth.

## Interesting Facts
Soybean produces more protein per hectare than any other major crop.
Despite being a legume, Soybean is primarily processed for its oil globally.
Tofu, soy milk, and soy sauce are all derived directly from the Soybean crop.
